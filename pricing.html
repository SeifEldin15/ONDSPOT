What is ONDSPOT?
ONDSPOT is a mobile on-demand car detailing service. We strive to provide the most convenient, reliable and transparent service to allow you to never have to worry about how you’re going to keep your car clean again. We bring the detail right to your doorstep, at the click of a button.
How do I book an appointment?
The process is simple. It starts with entering your address so we know where we will be detailing the vehicle, selecting the detailing package that best suits your needs,  followed by choosing a convenient day/time for you based on our availability for your area. Once the appointment is booked and confirmed, Your Detailer will come to your preferred location to provide the service on the day/time you requested. Rest assured, ONDSPOT securely handles your payment and chargers your card only after the service is completed, ensuring a secure transaction.
Do I need to call to get a quote first?
No you don’t! At ONDSPOT, we offer up-front and cost-effective pricing for our car detailing services. We have preset - fully transparent pricing via our select detail packages we offer. No matter the condition of your vehicle, there is a package that will fit your needs. If you are unsure which package to select, you can always contact us and we can recommend one for you based on your vehicle condition and what you want done :)
Do I need to book online?
All bookings are made online at . If you have a unique circumstance or would like someone on our team to book your appointment for you, please give us a call and we can assist you.
How do I pay?
Payment is done on the platform after entering your address, selecting your package, choosing the day/time of the appointment and providing your details. We accept both debit and credit cards for secure payment, ensuring utmost safety and security. Rest assured, ONDSPOT securely handles your payment and charges your card only after the service is completed, ensuring a secure transaction.
